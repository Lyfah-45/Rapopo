Our Mission: Sustainable Technology

At Electro, we believe in giving technology a second life. We specialize in recycled internal computer parts—from CPUs and motherboards to RAM and power supplies. Our goal is to reduce e-waste and make affordable, high-quality components available to everyone.

By collecting, testing, and repurposing parts from decommissioned systems, we’re helping the environment while supporting tech enthusiasts, builders, and students. When you buy from us, you're not just getting a great deal, you're supporting a greener future.

Join us in powering the next generation of sustainable computing!
